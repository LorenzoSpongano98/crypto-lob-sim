# ADR-0001: [WebSocket client engine selection]

<!-- Based on the MADR format. One decision per ADR, one to two pages. ADRs are immutable once accepted:
     to change a decision, write a new ADR that supersedes this one. Store them in docs/adr/. -->

## Context and Problem Statement

Engine selection for a WebSocket client, considering latency as a decision driver.

## Decision Drivers

- Latency and performance
- Ecosystem

## Considered Options

1. tokio-tungstenite
2. fastwebsockets(Deno)
3. tokio-websockets

## Decision Outcome

**Chosen option:** tokio-tungstenite in the current stage of the project, because of its consolidated ecosystem, despite not being the most performant. If latency and performance are issues in later stages of the project, consider switching to another engine

### Consequences

- **Positive:** consolidated engine ecosystem
- **Negative:** latency and performance may be an issue
- **Follow-up:** if latency and performance issue are present, revisit this ADR and consider switching to fastwebsockets or tokio-websockets
